# De-Paving for In-Ground Street Trees — Laws, Regulations & Best Practice

**Scope:** Ripping up impervious pavement ("despavimentació" / depaving) to create in-ground
tree pits for street/plaza trees. Focus order: Barcelona → Catalonia → Spain → EU →
transferable general practice.

**Date compiled:** 2026-05-31
**Convention:** Every factual claim carries a resolvable source + year. Claims I could not
verify against a primary source are tagged **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION** and must not be treated
as authoritative.

> Engineering note for the placement engine: an encodable decision rule is given in the final
> section ("Encodable Decision Rule"). The body below justifies each threshold.

---

## 0. Why de-paving (the goal)

De-paving converts sealed impervious surface back to permeable, plantable ground. In Barcelona
the practice is folded into two converging programmes: the **green-axes / superblock
(superilla)** transformation and **Sustainable Urban Drainage Systems (SUDS / SUDS-drenatge
urbà sostenible)**. The Almogàvers/Zamora green-axis works re-urbanised ~14,000 m² with
~15 of 20 m of street width given to footway, planting beds, trees and green space, and the
city's stated target is for **one in three Cerdà-grid streets to take this form by 2030**
([Ajuntament de Barcelona press, 2021](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/premsa/2021/10/20/barcelona-avanca-en-els-eixos-verds-de-sant-marti-amb-la-regeneracio-dels-carrers-dels-almogavers-i-de-zamora/)).
SUDS makes urban soil permeable to reproduce natural runoff, protect receiving waters and cut
flooding ([Barcelona Guia Tècnica de SUDS, Ajuntament de Barcelona](https://www.ajsosteniblebcn.cat/guia-suds_133931.pdf);
[Diputació de Barcelona SUDS infographic, BCNROC](https://bcnroc.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/jspui/handle/11703/139249)).

## 1. WHERE de-paving for in-ground tree pits is appropriate vs NOT

### Good candidates (de-pave → in-ground pit)
- **Wide sidewalks / footways** where, *after* the pit, the clear pedestrian path still meets
  the accessibility minimum (see §2). The accessibility order explicitly wants tree pits and
  grilles placed so they **do not invade the accessible pedestrian route**
  ([Orden TMA/851/2021, art. 12 & 18](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2021-13488)).
- **Parking/loading lanes and traffic-calmed carriageway edges** reclaimed in green-axis /
  superblock works — the dominant Barcelona de-paving context, where roadway is converted to
  planting + footway ([Ajuntament de Barcelona press, 2021](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/premsa/2021/10/20/barcelona-avanca-en-els-eixos-verds-de-sant-marti-amb-la-regeneracio-dels-carrers-dels-almogavers-i-de-zamora/)).
  Barcelona viari guidance permits pits in **slow lanes (<50 km/h), or ≥1.5 m from a fast lane
  (≥50 km/h)** ([Gestió de l'arbrat viari de Barcelona](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/ecologiaurbana/sites/default/files/Plagestioarbratviaribcn_cat.pdf), REQUIRES_VERIFICATION on the exact metres — extracted via search snippet, not confirmed against the PDF text).
- **Plaza / superblock-interior hardscape** that sits on natural ground (not over a basement
  slab) and is not heritage paving.
- **Sites with permeable subsoil and deep groundwater**, which also unlock SUDS infiltration
  pits (see §3).

### Poor / forbidden candidates (do NOT de-pave for in-ground)
- **Over underground utilities / metro / tunnels / buried mains.** Roots and excavation
  conflict with services; a pre-dig locate is mandatory (see §4). Where a main runs under the
  spot, the pit either moves or becomes a planter.
- **Over structural slabs: basements, underground car parks, podium decks, metro box.** There
  is no natural soil column; in-ground rooting is impossible and waterproofing/loading govern.
  Use contained soil-cell/planter systems instead (see §5)
  ([GreenBlue Urban — Planting Trees on Rooftops and Podium Decks](https://greenblue.com/na/planting-trees-on-rooftops-and-podium-decks/)).
- **Narrow sidewalks below the minimum clear width** once the pit is subtracted (see §2) —
  removing pavement there would breach accessibility law.
- **Heritage / protected paving** (catalogued pavements, monumental plazas). REQUIRES_VERIFICATION
  for the specific Barcelona heritage-paving catalogue/permit pathway.
- **Live road carriageways / fast lanes** without the setback above.
- **Steep grades and unstable/contaminated ground for *infiltration* pits.** Permeable-paving /
  infiltration guidance discourages slopes (>~2% poor for permeable systems) and capping of
  contamination should not be broken without a cleanup plan
  ([Habitat Network / Depave how-to, Cornell Lab](https://content.yardmap.org/learn/what-is-and-how-to-depave/) — search-snippet level, **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION** on exact slope figure;
  general SUDS siting in [susdrain "Using SuDS close to buildings", 2012](https://www.susdrain.org/files/resources/fact_sheets/09_12_fact_sheet_suds_close_to_buildings.pdf)).

## 2. Minimum dimensions / standards

### 2.1 Clear sidewalk width to KEEP after a pit (Spain, accessibility)
The governing national norm is **Orden TMA/851/2021** (the consolidated technical document on
basic accessibility of urbanised public space; it repealed and replaced Orden VIV/561/2010 from
2 Jan 2022).

- **Itinerario peatonal accesible — minimum free width: 1.80 m**, continuous along the route.
  ([Orden TMA/851/2021, art. 5.2.b](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2021-13488)).
- **Minimum free height: 2.20 m** (art. 5.2.c, same source).
- **Puntual narrowing:** the predecessor (Orden VIV/561/2010, art. 5.2) explicitly allowed, in
  exceptional consolidated-urban situations, a **reduction to no less than 1.50 m** at point
  obstacles ([Orden VIV/561/2010 consolidated, BOE](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2010-4057)).
  For the current order, treat **1.80 m as the design minimum** and only allow a 1.50 m
  pinch-point where the competent municipal accessibility authority accepts it.
  **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION** that TMA/851/2021 carries the same 1.50 m puntual allowance verbatim.
- **Tree pits & grilles in pedestrian areas:** must be **flush with the surrounding pavement**,
  and grille openings must not admit a circle larger than **1.6 cm diameter**
  ([Orden TMA/851/2021, art. 12](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2021-13488)).
- **Vegetation** must not obstruct the pedestrian passage zone or sightlines to signals
  (art. 18, same source). **Street furniture** sits at the outer (kerb) band, ≥40 cm from the
  kerb/roadway edge (art. 25.a, same source).

**Design corollary for the engine:** `usable_footway_after_pit = footway_width − pit_width`
must be `≥ 1.80 m` (hard), with `≥ 1.50 m` only as a flagged exception.

### 2.2 Minimum tree-pit size
- Common North-American street-tree practice: cut-outs **≥ 5 ft × 5 ft × 3 ft (≈1.5 × 1.5 × 0.9 m)**,
  larger preferred ([PSU Extension, construction-damaged soils](https://extension.psu.edu/understanding-tree-planting-in-construction-damaged-soils);
  Seattle Streets Illustrated §3.7](https://streetsillustrated.seattle.gov/design-standards/street-trees/)).
- **Rooting depth:** ~**90% of roots sit in the top 0.6 m**; **≥0.9 m (3 ft) of soil depth** is
  the windfirmness target ([PSU Extension](https://extension.psu.edu/understanding-tree-planting-in-construction-damaged-soils);
  [GreenBlue Urban](https://greenblue.com/na/planting-trees-on-rooftops-and-podium-decks/)).
- Barcelona-specific exact escocell minimum dimensions: **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION** — the city's
  *Guia de sembra, plantació i manteniment d'escocells* and the *Metodologia de treball:
  plantació d'arbres i palmeres* are the authoritative sources but their text could not be
  extracted here ([Guia escocells, Ajuntament de Barcelona](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/espaisverds/sites/default/files/2024-10/GuiaPlantacioEscocells.pdf);
  [Metodologia plantació arbres i palmeres](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/ecologiaurbana/sites/default/files/opos/Metodologia-treball-plantacio-arbres-palmeres.pdf)).
  A frequently cited working figure is a ~1.0 × 1.0 m open escocell — treat as
  **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION**.

### 2.3 Soil VOLUME per tree (the real constraint, more than footprint)
- **Industry rule-of-thumb minimums:** small ≈ **300 ft³ (8.5 m³)**, medium ≈ **600 ft³ (17 m³)**,
  large ≈ **1,000 ft³ (28 m³)** of soil per tree
  ([DeepRoot — Soil Volume Minimums by State/Province](https://www.deeproot.com/blog/blog-entries/soil-volume-minimums-organized-by-stateprovince-2/);
  [DeepRoot — Recommended Soil Volume](https://www.deeproot.com/blog/blog-entries/our-recommended-soil-volume-for-urban-trees-2/)).
  DeepRoot now argues large trees target ~**2,500 ft³ (~70 m³)**.
- Independent UK guidance gives **~25 m³ (large), ~15 m³ (medium), ~8 m³ (small)** good-quality
  soil, with **≥20 m³ at maturity** as a street-tree baseline
  ([Tree Pit Soil Volume Guidance v1.1, ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/341344046_Tree_Pit_Soil_Volume_Guidance_Version_11);
  [GreenBlue Urban — How Much Soil Do Urban Trees Need](https://greenblue.com/gb/how-much-soil-do-urban-trees-need/)).
- Several municipalities codify minimums (e.g. **Durham NC: 700/500/300 ft³** large/medium/small;
  **Tigard OR: up to 1,000 ft³**) ([DeepRoot — Municipalities with Soil Volume Minimums](https://www.deeproot.com/blog/blog-entries/municipalities-with-soil-volume-minimums-for-trees/)).

### 2.4 Open pit vs structural soil vs soil cells
- **Open soil** in a continuous bed is most efficient. Where soil must sit under load-bearing
  paving, use **structural soil** (load-bearing matrix that still aerates/hydrates) or
  **soil-cell/Silva-Cell systems**.
- **Conversion penalty:** structural (stone-skeleton) soil needs roughly **~4× the volume** to
  deliver the rootable soil of a soil-cell system, because most of its volume is stone
  ([Structural Soil Part 2, ASLA The Field, 2014](https://thefield.asla.org/2014/02/19/structural-soil-part-2/);
  [DeepRoot](https://www.deeproot.com/products/silva-cell/soil-volume-target/)). Barcelona has
  its own structural-soils design guide for Parks & Gardens
  ([Guia tècnica disseny de sòls estructurals, Ajuntament de Barcelona](https://bcnroc.ajuntament.barcelona.cat/jspui/handle/11703/142969)).

### 2.5 Pit spacing
- Spacing is set by mature canopy / available crown space, not a single fixed number. Diputació
  de Barcelona's tree property model classes **available space = distance from tree axis to the
  nearest urban obstacle**, banding from **2–2.5 m** (narrow crown) up to **>4.5 m** (very wide,
  >8 m crown) ([verd-urba.diba.cat — Propietats de l'arbrat](https://verd-urba.diba.cat/ajuda/arbrat)).
- Typical street-tree centre-to-centre spacing of **6–10 m** by species is conventional practice;
  the exact Barcelona figure is **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION** against the NTJ road-tree norm
  (**NTJ 01V** selection criteria; [APEVC NTJ catalogue](https://www.apevc.cat/serveis/ntj/)).

  infrastructure ([Guia Tècnica de SUDS, Ajuntament de Barcelona](https://www.ajsosteniblebcn.cat/guia-suds_133931.pdf);

## 4. Utilities — the mandatory pre-dig survey

**You cannot site an in-ground pit over buried mains from open data alone.** Open-data tree/
utility layers are indicative, not a dig clearance.

- **Locate before you dig is mandatory practice.** In North America this is the **811 / DigSafe**
  free locate, requested ~2 business days ahead; even planting holes must be located
  ([call811.com](https://811beforeyoudig.com/);
  [Davey — Planting near underground utilities](https://blog.davey.com/planting-trees-and-shrubs-near-underground-utility-lines/);
  [Sunshine 811 — Safe Planting](https://sunshine811.com/safe-planting)).
  Spain/Catalonia has no single 811 line; in practice the works promoter requests buried-service
  plans from each utility (water/Aigües de Barcelona, gas/sewer/electric/telecoms) and
  TMB/metro, and confirms with on-site detection (GPR / cable locator) before excavation —
  **REQUIRES_VERIFICATION** for the formal Catalan/Barcelona procedure name.
- **Setbacks from services (planting guidance, not a legal pit minimum):**
  - Trees/shrubs **≥10 ft (3 m)** from any utility service equipment/access box; small trees
    **10–15 ft (3–4.5 m)**, medium/large **≥20 ft (6 m)** from underground lines
    ([Davey](https://blog.davey.com/planting-trees-and-shrubs-near-underground-utility-lines/);
    [UtiliSource](https://utilisource.us/can-you-plant-trees-near-utility-lines/)).
  - Water/sewer: no single legal distance, but a practical **≥3 ft (≈1 m)** minimum and
    root-aware design, because sewers attract roots
    ([Casey Trees — Mythbusting Trees and Utilities, 2023](https://caseytrees.org/2023/09/mythbusting-trees-and-utilities/)).
- **Contamination check:** if removing pavement would uncap contaminated soil (common urban
  lead), do **not** de-pave without a cleanup plan — capping may be the safer state
  ([Depave / Habitat Network](https://content.yardmap.org/learn/what-is-and-how-to-depave/);
  [Shareable — How to free the soil by depaving](https://www.shareable.net/how-to/how-to-free-the-soil-by-depaving/)).

**Engine implication:** any candidate point within the utility buffer, or flagged
"unverified subsurface," is **not** in-ground-eligible at planning stage; it is either a
planter candidate or held pending a site locate.

## 5. When to use a PLANTER instead of de-pave + in-ground

Use an **above-ground / contained-root planter (or soil-cell system on slab)** instead of an
in-ground pit when in-ground rooting is impossible, unsafe, or non-compliant:

- **Over a basement, underground car park, podium deck or metro box** — no natural soil column;
  planting "cannot access natural water sources," so contained soil-cell systems are required;
  loading and waterproofing govern. Soil-cell systems can be as shallow as **~250 mm** build-up
  in engineered designs and carry heavy loads, but need irrigation and anchoring
  ([GreenBlue Urban — Rooftops & Podium Decks](https://greenblue.com/na/planting-trees-on-rooftops-and-podium-decks/);
  [rooflite — Trees on roofs / deep systems](https://www.rooflitesoil.com/blog/planting-trees-roofs-using-deep-systems/)).
- **Narrow walks** where de-paving would push clear width below **1.80 m** (or the accepted
  1.50 m pinch) ([Orden TMA/851/2021](https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2021-13488)).
- **Utility-dense spots** inside service setbacks (§4) where digging is unsafe.
- **Contaminated capped ground** without a remediation plan (§4).
- **Heritage paving** that cannot be cut (REQUIRES_VERIFICATION on local catalogue).

Planters trade lower establishment risk for **higher maintenance** (irrigation, smaller soil
volume → smaller mature tree, restricted species). Prefer in-ground wherever the rules above
allow it; default to planter only when an exclusion fires.

**Decision rule (plain language):**
> If the spot sits over a structural slab / basement / metro, OR within a utility setback, OR
> de-paving drops clear footway below the accessibility minimum, OR it is capped-contaminated or
> heritage paving → **planter (or no-plant)**. Otherwise, if soil-volume + spacing fit →
> **de-pave + in-ground pit**.

## 6. Encodable Decision Rule (for the placement engine)

Per candidate location, decide one of: `INGROUND_DEPAVE_OK` | `PLANTER_ONLY` | `NO_PLANT`.
Evaluate exclusions first (any fail → not in-ground); thresholds are explicit and tunable.

1. **Slab/void exclusion (hard):** if location is over a basement, underground car park, podium
   deck, or metro/tunnel box → **PLANTER_ONLY** (no natural soil). *(GreenBlue podium guidance.)*
2. **Utility exclusion (hard):** if location is within the utility buffer — default
   **≥1.0 m from water/sewer**, **≥2.0 m from gas/electric/telecom mains**, **≥3.0 m from
   service access boxes** (tune up to 6 m for large trees) — OR subsurface is "unverified" →
   not in-ground; **PLANTER_ONLY** if surface otherwise OK, else hold for site locate.
   *(Davey / Casey Trees; locate-before-dig is mandatory.)*
3. **Accessibility exclusion (hard):** require
   `footway_clear_width − pit_width ≥ 1.80 m`. If `< 1.80 m` but `≥ 1.50 m` → **PLANTER_ONLY**
   and flag "needs accessibility waiver." If `< 1.50 m` → **NO_PLANT** on footway.
   *(Orden TMA/851/2021 art. 5; VIV/561/2010 1.50 m puntual.)*
4. **Heritage/road exclusion (hard):** heritage-catalogued paving → **NO_PLANT** (or planter if
   allowed); live fast-lane carriageway with **< 1.5 m** setback from a ≥50 km/h lane →
   **NO_PLANT**. Slow-lane (<50 km/h) edges are eligible. *(BCN viari guidance — VERIFY metres.)*
5. **Contamination exclusion (hard):** if soil is flagged capped-contaminated and no remediation
   plan → **NO_PLANT** (do not break the cap). *(Depave guidance.)*
6. **Pit footprint minimum:** require open pit **≥ 1.5 × 1.5 m** surface (≈5×5 ft); smaller →
   downgrade to small-tree species or **PLANTER_ONLY**. *(PSU / Seattle.)*
7. **Soil-volume gate (species-dependent):** require rootable soil
   **≥ 8.5 m³ (small) / 17 m³ (medium) / 28 m³ (large)**; if achievable only via structural
   soil, multiply required volume by **~4×**. If volume can't be met in-ground but can in a
   connected soil-cell trench → **PLANTER_ONLY (soil cells)**. *(DeepRoot; ASLA structural soil.)*
8. **Depth gate:** require **≥ 0.9 m** rootable depth for medium/large; if only shallow build-up
   (~0.25–0.6 m, e.g. over slab) → **PLANTER_ONLY** with shallow soil-cell system + small species.
   *(PSU; GreenBlue.)*
9. **Spacing gate:** enforce mature-crown clearance — distance from trunk axis to nearest
   obstacle/other tree per crown band: **≥2.0–2.5 m** (narrow) … **≥4.5 m** (>8 m crown);
   default centre-to-centre **6–10 m** (VERIFY against NTJ 01V). Too tight → drop point or pick
   narrower species. *(verd-urba.diba.cat; NTJ — VERIFY.)*
10. **Infiltration/SUDS gate (only if pit doubles as SUDS):** slope **≤ ~2%**, soil infiltration
    **≥ 1×10⁻⁶ m/s**, **≥1 m unsaturated ground above groundwater**, and **≥5 m from building/
    road foundations** for an infiltrating feature; else use lined pit (no infiltration claim).
    *(susdrain SuDS-close-to-buildings, 2012; general SuDS standards.)*
11. **Outcome:** if all hard exclusions pass AND gates 6–9 pass → **INGROUND_DEPAVE_OK**
    (apply SUDS gate 10 if infiltration is intended). If a hard exclusion fired but surface is
    plantable → **PLANTER_ONLY**. Otherwise → **NO_PLANT**.
12. **Confidence/verification flag:** every `INGROUND_DEPAVE_OK` carries
    `requires_site_locate = true` until a real utility locate confirms clearance — open-data
    layers never satisfy gate 2 on their own. *(Locate-before-dig is mandatory.)*
